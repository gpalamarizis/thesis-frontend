Frequently asked questions

Is the application in Greek?

Yes. Screens, lists and forms are in Greek. Fee statements and invoices to clients can also be produced in English or French. An English user interface is in preparation.

Does it connect to iJustice?

Not yet: iJustice has not opened a connection for third-party software. Thesis keeps all your hearings and deadlines in one place, and you file through iJustice as you do today.

Where is my data stored?

The Cyprus database is separate from the Greek one and hosted in a data centre in the EU (Amsterdam). Each firm sees only its own data and can export it at any time.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser on Windows, Mac and tablets. All you need is an internet connection.

How does billing work?

Annually, per user, plus 19% VAT. The monthly figure is shown for comparison only. Pay by card through Viva or by bank transfer.

Can I bring data from my old system?

Yes. We migrate cases, clients and history from older software or from Excel, on quotation.
